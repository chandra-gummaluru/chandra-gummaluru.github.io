Bayesian Network Independence Widget

D-Separation Visualization

Understanding independence relationships in Bayesian networks is crucial for probabilistic reasoning. This interactive widget helps visualize d-separation and conditional independence in graphical models, allowing students to explore how information flows through probabilistic networks.

How to Use

Interact with the widget above to explore independence relationships in Bayesian networks. Select nodes to query their independence relationships and see how conditioning on evidence affects the network structure.
