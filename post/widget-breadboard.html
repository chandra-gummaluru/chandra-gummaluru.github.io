Breadboard Lab

Wiring Circuits by Hand

A breadboard for the lab bench, without the lab bench. Place chips, LEDs and resistors, run wires between the rows, flip the switches on the board, and probe a node to see what it reads. The rails behave like real ones, so a wire in the wrong row does exactly what it would in the room.

How to Use

  • Pick a tool from the toolbar: wire, LED, resistor, chip, or the probe.
  • Click two holes to run a wire between them, keeping the power rails in mind.
  • Turn the power on, then use the switches to drive your inputs and watch the output LEDs.
  • Use the probe to read the voltage at any node, and Clear to start over.

The widget runs on its own in any browser, and it also drops straight onto a slide in Beamer+.
