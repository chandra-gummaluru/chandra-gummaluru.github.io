Circuit Simulator

Build Logic Circuits and Watch Them Run

A logic circuit you can build a piece at a time. Drop in sources, gates, flip-flops and displays, wire the pins together, then flip an input and watch the signal travel through the circuit, one gate delay at a time, instead of appearing all at once.
