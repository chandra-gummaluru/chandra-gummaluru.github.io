Circuit Simulator

Build Logic Circuits and Watch Them Run

A logic circuit you can build a piece at a time. Drop in sources, gates, flip-flops and displays, wire the pins together, then flip an input and watch the signal travel through the circuit, one gate delay at a time, instead of appearing all at once.

How to Use

  • Click or drag a part from the palette on the left to place it on the board.
  • Click one pin and then another to wire them together.
  • Click an input to toggle it between 0 and 1, and watch the signal propagate.
  • Use the speed control to slow the propagation down, and switch between binary, decimal and hexadecimal.
  • Export a circuit to a file to save it, or import one you built earlier.

The widget runs on its own in any browser, and it also drops straight onto a slide in Beamer+.
